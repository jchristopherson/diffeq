VODE-based variable-order ODE solvers.

VODE uses variable-step formulas of the general form where the coefficient set and order change with the selected method. Adams formulas are intended primarily for non-stiff systems; backward differentiation formulas are intended primarily for stiff systems. The wrapper manages VODE's work arrays, tolerances, and optional Jacobian callback.
