adams

Explicit predictor, implicit corrector (PECE) multi-step solvers.

An Adams method advances the solution by integrating an interpolating polynomial fitted to the derivative history, The predictor fits that polynomial to derivatives already known, giving an explicit Adams-Bashforth formula, while the corrector includes the derivative at the new point as well, giving an implicit Adams-Moulton formula of the same order. Evaluating the two in sequence, and taking their difference as a measure of the local error, is the classical PECE cycle. Each step therefore costs exactly two derivative evaluations and requires neither a Jacobian nor the solution of a linear system.
